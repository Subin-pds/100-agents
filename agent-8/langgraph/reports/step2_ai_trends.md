### Top 5 Durable Trends

1.  **The Rise of AI Agents and Agentic Systems:** The increasing focus on agent frameworks, protocols for agent interactions (especially financial), and agentic development platforms signifies a fundamental shift towards more autonomous and interconnected AI.
2.  **Specialized AI Infrastructure and Observability:** As AI systems move into production, the need for dedicated tools to manage, monitor, and ensure the reliability of AI-native infrastructure, including new metrics for observability, is becoming paramount.
3.  **Cloud Provider Domination in AI Ecosystems:** Major cloud providers continue to be central to AI development, offering not only foundational models and compute but also critical certifications and platform-specific tools that define industry standards.
4.  **Practical, Applied AI Engineering Skills:** The demand is heavily weighted towards engineers who can not only understand AI models but also effectively implement, optimize, evaluate, and deploy them in real-world, cost-efficient scenarios.
5.  **Security and Trust in AI Systems:** The emergence of "Know Your Agent" frameworks and dedicated security solutions for AI agents underscores the growing importance of building secure, trustworthy, and auditable AI systems.
